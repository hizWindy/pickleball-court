# Desk routine

For the host. The booking site only knows what is on the desk (`/admin`). These four habits keep two groups from ever
being sent to the same court.

## Every day

1. **Enter every walk-in and phone booking the moment it's agreed.**
   Add booking → *Walk-in or call*. Until it is on the desk, the site can sell those hours to someone online.

2. **Tap Arrived when the group is on court.**
   On the Schedule it is the *Here* button on the booking; in Bookings it is *Arrived*. It tells the system the court is
   in use.

3. **Act on Late prompts.**
   Fifteen minutes after the start without *Arrived*, the booking turns **Late**. The desk shows it (and Telegram pings
   you), and the court stays theirs until you choose:
   - they're playing, you just hadn't tapped it → **Arrived**;
   - they're not coming → **Release court**. The hours go back on sale. Changed your mind? **Restore** within 5
     minutes, if nobody booked them meanwhile.

4. **Never hand out a court without adding it on the desk first.**
   Even for half an hour: add it, then hand it out.

## When it happens

- **Paid late** (red): a payment came in after the player's time ran out, and someone else had already booked the slot.
  The player was told you'll be in touch. Check the payment in your app, then call or text them: move it (*Edit* to a
  free time, then *Reopen and confirm*) or refund it.
- **Rain delay**: the guests' hours stay closed until each one picks a new time on the site with the link you send. Tap
  **Open hours** on a booking if you'd rather sell those hours sooner.
- **Stuck?** Every booking's *History* says what happened and when.
